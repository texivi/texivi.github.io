What TempleOS is to me

Terry Davis made an operating system by himself, with a shell that is also its compiler, in one language, small enough for one person to understand. I don't know of another project that does that.

Whatever else is said about it, that part is true, and it is the part I took. My system has none of its code, and a different language, but it asks the same question: what can one person build if they decide everything?

I wanted to say so here in my own words, because it is the reason I started.
