Fluent

Fluent is a Mac app I write in Swift. It has typing practice, aim and click tests, lessons for trackpad gestures and keyboard shortcuts, and a checklist for looking over a used Mac.

It is free and open source. The source is at github.com/fluent-mac/fluent and the app can be downloaded from fluent-mac.github.io. It needs macOS 26 or later.
