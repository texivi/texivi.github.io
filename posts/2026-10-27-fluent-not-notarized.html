Why Fluent is not notarized

Fluent is not notarized. Notarization needs a paid Apple Developer account, and I have not set one up for it yet.

Because of that, macOS may block the app the first time you open it. Go to System Settings, Privacy & Security and click Open Anyway. After that it opens normally.

The build setup on GitHub already has the signing and notarization steps. They run once the account secrets are added, and the app will open without the prompt after that. The source is at github.com/fluent-mac/fluent.
