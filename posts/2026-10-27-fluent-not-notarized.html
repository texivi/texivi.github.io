Why Fluent is not notarized

Fluent is not notarized. Notarization requires a paid Apple Developer account, which I have not set up yet.

As a result, macOS may block the app the first time you open it. Open System Settings, go to Privacy & Security and click Open Anyway. After that it opens normally.

The build setup on GitHub already contains the signing and notarization steps. They run once the account secrets are added, and the prompt will no longer appear. The source is at github.com/fluent-mac/fluent.
