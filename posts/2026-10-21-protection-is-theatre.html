Protection is theatre

Subverge has no users and no permissions. I could have implemented them, and I chose not to.

Privilege rings exist because a general-purpose system has to keep working for people who depend on it every day, and one faulty program must not take the rest down. Nobody depends on Subverge for work, and nothing is lost when it crashes. Protection would cost design effort and give nothing back.

This is not a criticism of Linux. I think it is excellent, and I run Exherbo myself. My point is aimed at other hobby OS developers: your system is unlikely to be used every day, and that gives you room to experiment. Spend the effort on the idea. See A hobby OS is allowed to be impractical and Not a daily driver for more.
