Subverge 0.9.5

Subverge 0.9.5 can show pictures. The kernel has a JPEG decoder, graphics mode is 24-bit colour now, and gallery goes through nine pictures built into the system.

Programs can make their own: bitmap gives a pixel grid, bmp-set and bmp-box change it and bmp-show draws it. The credits are a command now too, credits, with a page each for me, gretagen and Vsauce.

The release and the notes are on the Subverge site.
