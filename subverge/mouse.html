Mouse

The mouse works on the text screen if QEMU gives the system a PS/2 mouse. The pointer is one character cell with it's colours swapped, and it moves one cell at a time. The PS/2 controller describes how the mouse talks to the system.

What the mouse does

  • A click on a tab title in the top bar switches to that tab.
  • In the help viewer, a click follows a link or runs a line of code, the wheel scrolls and a right click goes back (help).

Words

Word Effect
mouse -- x y buttons the pointer cell and the buttons
mouse? -- 1/0 1 when a mouse was found at boot

x and y count from the top-left cell of the area of the tab, so y is one less than the screen row, and y is -1 over the top bar. buttons is the sum of 1 for the left button, 2 for the right button and 4 for the middle button:

mouse? .            1
mouse . . .         buttons, then y, then x

A mouse with a wheel is found at boot. A mouse that does not answer is left unused, and mouse? is 0. In QEMU the pointer follows the mouse of your computer after the first click in the QEMU window, and Ctrl-Alt-G lets go of it.
