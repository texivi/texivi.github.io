Part 6: Tabs

There are four shells. The top line has a title for each of them, and a * after a title means that shell printed something while you were elsewhere. F1 to F4 and Alt-1 to Alt-4 show a shell, and a click on a title with the mouse does too. Each shell has it is own stack, it's own definitions and its own history.

Start something that runs for a while:

0 100000000 [ 1 + ] times .

Press F2 to show shell 2 and type the line there, then press F1. Shell 2 goes on computing while you are in shell 1, and its title gets a * when it prints. Ctrl-C in a shell stops the line it is running.

See tabs, keys and scheduler.
