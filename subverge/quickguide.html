Subverge / Handbook

Keys
F1 to F4, Alt-1 to Alt-4 Tabs 1 to 4
Ctrl-C Stop the running line
Tab Complete a name at the prompt
Up, Down Earlier lines

In the help viewer, Up, Down, PgUp, PgDn, Home and End scroll, Space pages down, Tab selects the next link, Enter follows it, Backspace goes back and q or Esc leaves. Enter on a line of code closes the viewer and runs the line at the prompt. docs opens the viewer at the index.
