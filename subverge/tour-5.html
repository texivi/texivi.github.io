Part 5: Flow, lists and strings

Conditions and loops take quotes:

5 3 > [ "bigger" say ] [ "smaller" say ] if
10 [ "hi" say ] times
0 5 [ . ] for
3 [ dup 0 > ] [ dup . 1 - ] while drop

A quote is also a list:

0 5 range .
[ 1 2 3 ] [ sq ] map .
[ 1 2 3 4 ] [ 2 mod 0 = ] filter .
[ 3 1 2 ] len .

Strings:

"subverge" upper say
"a,b,c" "," split .
"x = " 42 str cat say

See flow, quotes and strings. Every word on these lines has a page. Click a name to read it: if, times, for, while, range, map, filter, split, cat.
