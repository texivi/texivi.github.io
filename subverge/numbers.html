Subverge / Handbook

Numbers

Integers are exact. Up to 64 bits they are stored directly, and a result that does not fit becomes a bignum of up to 2^65536. For infinite values, see ordinals and cardinals.

Literals

42   -7   0x2a   0b101010   -0x10

You can write integers in decimal, in hex with 0x, or in binary with 0b, with an optional minus sign. Hex digits can be upper or lower case. A number that is too big for 64 bits is read as a bignum:

123456789012345678901234567890 .

bases prints a number in decimal, hex, octal and binary (tools).

Arithmetic

Word Effect
+ a b -- c adds
- a b -- c subtracts
* a b -- c multiplies
/ a b -- c divides, rounding toward zero
mod a b -- r the remainder, with the sign of a
^ a b -- c a to the power b
neg a -- -a flips the sign
abs a -- b drops the sign
min a b -- c the smaller
max a b -- c the bigger
sqrt n -- r the square root, rounded down
gcd a b -- c the greatest common divisor
inc n -- n+1 adds one
dec n -- n-1 subtracts one
sq n -- n*n squares

All of them accept bignums. +, * and ^ also work on ordinals and cardinals, which have their own rules.

17 5 /          3
-17 5 /         -3
-17 5 mod       -2
2 10 ^          1024
10 sqrt         3
84 36 gcd       12

Division and remainders

/ rounds toward zero and mod takes the sign of the number being divided, the same as in C. Two things catch people out:

  • A test for odd numbers written as 2 mod 1 = is false for negative odd numbers, because -3 2 mod is -1. Write 2 mod 0 = not instead.
  • Turning a negative number of seconds into minutes with / rounds toward zero, not down.

Dividing by zero fails. Dividing the most negative 64-bit integer by -1 gives a bignum instead of overflowing.

Powers and roots

^ raises a number to a whole power that is not negative, exactly. Negative powers fail. A power whose result would be larger than 2^65536 fails before it is worked out, so 2 70000 ^ fails at once instead of running out of memory.

sqrt rounds down and works on bignums: 2 200 ^ sqrt is exactly 2 100 ^. Negative numbers fail. gcd always gives a result that is not negative, and 0 0 gcd is 0.

Big numbers

Integers never wrap around. A result that does not fit in 64 bits becomes a bignum, and a bignum result that fits again becomes a plain integer:

2 100 ^ .                   1267650600228229401496703205376
2 100 ^ 2 100 ^ - .         0
2 1000 ^ str len .          302

Your code does not see the switch. Every arithmetic and comparison word accepts both kinds, and so do str, >num, min, max, conditions and saving. Compiled code checks for overflow after every addition, subtraction and multiplication, and calls the bignum code when it happens (JIT).

Words that need a machine integer, such as times, at, peek, alloc and the bitwise words, fail with name needs a number that fits in 64 bits. In a condition a bignum is always true, because a bignum is never zero.

The limit is 2^65536, which is about 19,700 decimal digits. A larger result fails. See bignums for how they are stored and calculated.

Comparisons and truth

Word Effect
= a b -- 1/0 equal
< > <= >= a b -- 1/0 ordering
and or a b -- 1/0 logical
not a -- 1/0 logical not

Comparisons leave 1 or 0. Conditions treat 0 as false and every other number as true. and and or look at both sides, because both are already on the stack, and they always leave 1 or 0.

= works on any two values: numbers by value, strings by content, and everything else by identity. The ordering words accept numbers, ordinals and cardinals.

Bits

Word Effect
& a b -- c and
\| a b -- c or
xor a b -- c exclusive or
~ a -- b not
<< a n -- b shift left
>> a n -- b shift right, filling with zeros

The bitwise words work on 64-bit integers only. Shift counts are taken modulo 64, and >> is a logical shift, so -1 60 >> is 15. Remember that and is logical and & works on bits: 12 10 and is 1, but 12 10 & is 8.

Random numbers

n rand gives a number from 0 to n-1. It uses a xorshift generator that is seeded from the cycle counter of the processor at the first call. Use it for games and shuffling, not for secrets, because its whole state is one 64-bit number. rand fails for zero or less.

6 rand 1 + .            a dice roll

Converting

str and hex turn numbers into text, >hex adds the 0x, and >num reads a number back from a string (strings). fmt pads a number to fit a column.

Fractions

Floating point numbers are on the roadmap. Until then, write fractions as fixed point: multiply by a scale, keep integers, and divide when you print.

1999 3 * .                  5997, which is 59.97 at a scale of 100
5997 100 / .                59
5997 100 mod .              97

The bundled programs do this: the unit conversions in convert work in hundredths, and mandel uses 65536 as 1.0. calc is a separate calculator that works with six decimal places (tools).
